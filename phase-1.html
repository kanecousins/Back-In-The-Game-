Phase 1

Settle & activate

Settle symptoms and start hip and trunk work without lumbar extension load. Sport is paused and provocative extension loading is avoided.

The three most important goals

1Settle symptoms and restore daily function
2Learn neutral-spine trunk control
3Athlete and parents understand the plan and the pain rules
Start when
Working or confirmed diagnosis, red flags excluded, an experienced physiotherapist and the medical team aware, and any slip excluded or graded.

Exercise prescription

Focus Examples
Daily function Walking as tolerated; avoid provocative positions
Trunk, static holds Isometric holds in neutral spine: modified curl-up, side bridge (from knees), bird-dog. Short holds, technique over volume
Rotation, controlled Thoracic rotation (open book), hip rotation, and small-range pain-free trunk rotation avoiding lumbar extension
Hips Hip flexor stretch, glute activation, hip hinge patterning
Education Explain accurately without alarm; teach the pain rules

Active lumbar extension and the stork test are retested at Phase 3 entry, not Phase 1. Prone control is observed, replacing the protocols’ pressure-cuff gate (>10 s, ≤4 mmHg drop). Pain is tracked as a symptom response (see Rehabilitate overview), not repeated as a gate. Several gates come from Mass General Brigham (2024), Sanford (2024) or a case report (Brearley et al., 2021). None is validated for this condition.

About the Micheli Functional Scale

A short questionnaire the athlete completes, developed to measure low back pain and function in young athletes (d’Hemecourt et al., 2012). It has five items: a symptom question (scored 0–5), difficulty with extension (0–4), flexion (0–3) and jumping (0–3) activities, and pain on a 0–10 scale.

Scoring: add the four item scores (0–15) to the pain score (0–10), then multiply by 4 to give a score from 0 to 100%. Higher scores mean more limitation; 0% means no limitation.

When to use it: at baseline (Phase 1), at Phase 3 entry and at Phase 4, where 0% is a return to sport criterion. For the questionnaire and full scoring, see the original paper.
