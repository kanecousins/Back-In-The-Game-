Five messages to take with you

  • 1. Scan with care. Pars defects are common without symptoms; read imaging with the clinical picture.
  • 2. Normal X-ray is not the all-clear. Discuss referral with your supervisor and medical team.
  • 3. Words matter. Be accurate, without being alarming.
  • 4. Start early, progress by criteria. Modify sport and load, while following the plan.
  • 5. Gates are partly borrowed. Most come from protocols or clinical judgement; see About.

Evidence is mostly Level III–IV. One RCT (n = 64, ages 10–19, MRI-confirmed). No validated return-to-sport battery.
