Phase 4

Full training & competition

Return to unrestricted training, then graded competition, with a plan for recurrence. In the 2026 trial, recurrence needing medical care within 12 months was 3% (immediate physiotherapy) and 29% (rest first).

The three most important goals

1Tolerate full training without a reaction
2Meet all three Return to Sport checklists
3Agree who to contact if symptoms return
Start when
All Phase 3 boxes ticked, the coach has the written plan, and Return to Sport checklists are in progress.

Exercise prescription

Focus Examples
Full training Build to full sessions over at least 2 weeks; unrestricted skills
Graded competition Limit minutes or events, then build; one variable at a time
Maintenance Continue strength and trunk work 2–3 times per week; review at agreed intervals
