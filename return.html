Step 4

Return to Sport

Clearance needs all three domains. No validated battery exists.

1 · Physical

  • 2 weeks pain-free return activity
  • Micheli Functional Scale 0%
  • Full training tolerated, no next-day reaction

2 · Psychological

  • Athlete confident back will cope
  • No marked fear of re-injury
  • Athlete helped set the plan
  • Knows what to do if symptoms return

3 · Stakeholders

  • Athlete, parents and coach each briefed
  • Shared understanding of criteria
  • Written first-competition plan
  • Everyone knows who to contact

Protocols: 12+ weeks to sport-specific return, 3–6 months to expected healing, longer if bilateral (MGH, 2024; Sanford, 2024).
