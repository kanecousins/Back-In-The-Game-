Phase 3

Sport-specific reintroduction

Reintroduce sport movement, including graded extension and rotation, at sub-maximal then sport-demand load. Change one variable at a time.

The three most important goals

1Return to extension and rotation, step by step
2Rebuild sport technique and workload
3Build confidence for sport movement
Start when
All Phase 2 boxes ticked, the athlete feels ready to begin sport-related movement, active lumbar extension and the stork test have been retested, and the Micheli Functional Scale repeated.

Exercise prescription

Focus Examples
Graded exposure Unloaded and controlled first; add load, speed and range; re-test pain at each step
Sport drills Technique-focused drills building toward sport-demand workloads
Strength and power Progressive resistance; low-level plyometrics; running progression if relevant
Technique and load Coach and physiotherapist review technique and load together

Bonus goals (optional, not required to move on)

Bonus goal Test and how to read it Goal Done

Bonus goals do not count toward moving on. They are borrowed from protocols or clinical judgement and are not validated for this condition.

Moving the extension and stork tests here from Phase 1 departs from the protocols reviewed. No spondylolysis-specific criteria exist for drill intensity or volume.
