Phase 2

Build capacity

Build strength and fitness with progressive load and no lumbar extension. Judge each session by the next morning.

The three most important goals

1Build trunk endurance (60-second holds) and flexion strength
2Build lower-body strength 2–3 times per week
3Raise conditioning with no next-day reaction
Start when
All Phase 1 boxes ticked and no reaction the morning after the last session.

Exercise prescription

Focus Examples
Trunk, endurance Progress curl-up, side bridge and bird-dog to front plank, side plank and prone extension hold; anti-rotation work
Trunk, flexion Progress curl-up to full curl-up; dead bug, reverse curl, bent-knee leg lowering, ball roll-out or hanging knee raise. Controlled, no hip flexor dominance
Lower body Bridge to single-leg bridge; squat, split squat, step-up; loaded hinge in neutral spine
Conditioning Upright cycling or elliptical; add duration, then intensity
Avoid End-range or loaded lumbar extension, impact, sport-specific provocative positions

The 60-second holds come from one protocol (Sanford, 2024). Adolescent reference values differ by sex and age, so interpret holds with clinical judgement.
